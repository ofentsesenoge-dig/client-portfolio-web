About

I'm a final-year Bachelor of Computer and Information Sciences student specialising in Application Development at Emeris (formerly IIE Varsity College), based in Pretoria, South Africa. I expect to complete my studies in 2026 and graduate in 2027.

I completed my Software Development Internship at FOCI Group in October 2026, gaining practical experience in API testing, regression testing, performance and load testing with k6, and backend validation using PostgreSQL and Supabase. Beyond technology, I'm a U23 South African Cross Triathlon Champion who competes nationally and internationally. Balancing competitive sport with university and software development has taught me discipline, consistency, and how to work towards long-term goals.

I'm interested in opportunities where I can contribute to building useful software, strengthen my engineering skills, and grow as a developer.
